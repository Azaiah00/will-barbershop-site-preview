Why do some prices have a plus sign?

A "+" means the price starts there. Longer hair, extra detail or add-ons can raise the final price; ask your barber before the cut.

How much do designs or bleach cost?

Designs, part lines and bleach or platinum color are priced at the chair because every job is different. Call (804) 909-9210 or ask when you come in.

How do I book?

Book online on Booksy, call or text (804) 909-9210, or just walk in.
