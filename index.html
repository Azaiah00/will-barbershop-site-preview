FAQ

Before
you sit down

Straight answers to what people ask us most.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes. Walk-ins are welcome every day. If you want a guaranteed time, book your spot on Booksy before you come.

How much is a haircut at Will Barbershop?

A regular haircut starts at $40, haircut and beard starts at $50, and the 90-minute master haircut is $100. Kid haircuts are $40.

What are your hours?

We are open seven days a week, 9 AM to 8 PM. Sunday hours can be shorter, so check live availability on Booksy or call (804) 909-9210.

Where are you located and where do I park?

904 W Leigh St, Richmond, VA 23220, a few blocks from VCU. The shop lists a parking space on Booksy; if it is taken, use nearby street parking and follow posted signs.

Do you do house calls?

Yes. Book the "House cut" on Booksy ($150) and Will comes to you.

Can I get a haircut after 7 PM?

Yes, by appointment. Booksy has an "After 7:00 PM" booking that starts at $100.

Do the barbers speak Spanish?

Sí. Will Barbershop is run by Dominican barbers, and you can talk to us in English or Spanish.

Do you accept credit cards?

Yes. Credit cards are accepted.
